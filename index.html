WHERE THE BOUNDARY IS

A glimpse, not your whole identity.

Requires extra access

Precise geolocation, camera, microphone, screen capture, and access to local files or clipboard contents are gated by permissions, user actions or browser restrictions. This page does not request them, even if you previously granted access.

Cannot be read directly

Your name, email, passwords, full browsing history, MAC address and device serial number are not normally exposed to an ordinary page. A site may learn your identity if you sign in or provide it.

Clues can add up

Other sites can combine browser, screen and hardware signals into a fingerprint. A VPN changes the network address a site sees; it does not hide every browser signal. Values may be shared, spoofed or deliberately limited.

How this display works. Browser readings stay in this page's memory. No visitor profile, fingerprint ID, analytics event or database record is created by this display. IP and country come from this site's Cloudflare diagnostic endpoint. Hosting providers and the existing Google Fonts service still receive normal web requests and may keep their own logs.

Country is approximate; a VPN, proxy or mobile network can put it elsewhere. City, street address, precise coordinates and ISP are not supplied by this lookup. “Not exposed” means unavailable, unsupported or withheld — not that the feature does not exist.
